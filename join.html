Join us

We welcome students who are curious about how genomes work and willing to learn to code. You do not need programming experience to start.

Undergraduates

Howard undergraduates can join through BIOL 390 Directed Independent Study, an honors thesis, or a summer project. Taking BIOL 419 (Bioinformatics) or DATA 405 / BIOL 480 (Genomic Data Science) first is helpful but not required.

Graduate students

Ph.D. and master's students in Biology or Data Science interested in computational genomics, evolution or machine learning for biology are encouraged to arrange a rotation.

Postdocs

Postdoctoral applicants with experience in genomics, statistics or deep learning should email a CV and a short statement of research interests.

How to get in touch

Email jinrui.xu@howard.edu with a few sentences about your background, what interests you about our research, and your CV or unofficial transcript.
