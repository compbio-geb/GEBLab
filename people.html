Jinrui Xu, Ph.D.

Principal Investigator
Assistant Professor, Department of Biology and Center for Applied Data Science & Analytics, Howard University
Pronounced Jin-ray Hsu

Jinrui is a computational biologist who studies genome evolution and gene regulation. He earned his Ph.D. in Bioinformatics at the University of Michigan with Jianzhi Zhang, then spent eight years in Computational Biology and Bioinformatics at Yale University as a postdoctoral associate and associate research scientist, contributing to ENCODE, EN-TEx and GENCODE. He joined Howard in 2023. He was a Brookhaven National Laboratory Visiting Faculty in 2025 and 2026.
