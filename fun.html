Designing our logo

October 2026

Our logo tells the story of Geb in one picture. The gold arch is Nut, the sky, bending over the earth with stars on her body.

Beneath her stands a goose, the sign used to write the name of Geb, ancient Egyptian god of the earth. It stands on a DNA double helix: for us, the genome is the ground every organism grows from. Lapis blue and gold were the colors of Egyptian jewelry.
